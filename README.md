# Customer Engagement & Product Utilization Analytics for Retention Strategy

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer engagement behavior, product utilization patterns, and relationship strength indicators to understand customer churn and retention behavior in the banking sector.

The dashboard was developed using Microsoft Power BI and focuses on engagement-driven retention analytics rather than demographic analysis alone.

---

## Technologies Used

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Microsoft Excel

---

## Key Features

* Engagement vs Churn Analysis
* Product Utilization Analysis
* High-Value Customer Risk Detection
* Relationship Strength Index (RSI)
* Retention Strength Dashboard
* Interactive Filters and Slicers
* Scatter Plot Diagnostics
* KPI Tracking

---

## Key Insights

* Inactive customers show significantly higher churn rates
* Single-product customers are more likely to churn
* Multi-product customers demonstrate stronger retention
* High-balance customers may still churn due to low engagement
* Weak RSI customers contribute disproportionately to churn

---

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — Engagement vs Churn

Analyzes customer activity and churn behavior.

### Page 2 — Product Utilization

Evaluates product depth and customer loyalty.

### Page 3 — High-Value At-Risk Customers

Identifies premium customers with high churn risk.

### Page 4 — Retention Strength

Measures relationship strength using RSI scoring.
